### Key Finding

**FY1973 shows NO significant departure from Apollo-era linguistic patterns.**

The hedging density of 4.5/1k is:
- Within the Apollo baseline range (1.7-5.6)
- Above the FY1970-1972 nadir (1.7-1.8), but this variation is normal
- **Far below modern aerospace levels (NOC 2024: 15.5/1k)**

### Implication for Variance Compression Thesis

The +800% hedging inflation observed in modern aerospace documents **did not occur immediately post-Apollo**. This supports the hypothesis that variance compression is:

1. **Gradual institutional drift**, not disaster response
2. Not triggered by Apollo 1 fire (no effect in FY1968)
3. Not triggered by program conclusion (FY1973 stable)

### Inflection Point Hypothesis

The inflection point likely occurs somewhere between:
- **FY1973** (4.5/1k — Apollo-era stable)
- **FY2024** (15.5/1k — modern compressed)
