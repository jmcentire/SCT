KEY FINDINGS FOR VARIANCE COMPRESSION THESIS
============================================

1. INFLECTION POINT IDENTIFIED: FY1976-1977
   The major linguistic shift occurred in the post-Apollo, pre-Shuttle
   transition period - NOT gradually over decades.

2. PEAK HEDGING WAS IN 1977, NOT TODAY
   FY1977 (9.8/1k) remains the highest hedging in 66 years.
   Modern NASA documents (6.5/1k mean) are actually ~34% LOWER.

3. DISASTERS SHOW NO LASTING LINGUISTIC EFFECT
   Confirms original hypothesis: hedging is institutional, not event-driven.
   Both Challenger and Columbia showed no sustained linguistic shift.

4. NOC 2024 COMPARISON (15.5/1k) IS ANOMALOUS
   If the comparison aerospace document showed 15.5/1k, it represents
   either a different document type, contractor language, or industry
   source - not comparable to NASA Congressional budget justifications.

5. NOMINALIZATION IS THE REAL DRIFT
   While hedging plateaued, nominalization shows steady increase.
   This may be the better metric for "bureaucratization."
