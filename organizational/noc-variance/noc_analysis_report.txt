METHODOLOGY
-----------
Following the methodology established in "The Cage" framework analysis:
- Lexical Diversity (LD): Unique tokens / Total tokens
- Shannon Entropy (SE): Information unpredictability (base-2)
- Hedging Density: Epistemic markers per 1000 words
- Passive Voice: Passive constructions per 100 sentences
- Nominalization: Noun-derived-from-verb density per 1000 words

KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------
1. LEXICAL DIVERSITY: Minimal net compression (-0.4% over 9 years)
   - NOC maintains LD ~0.17, comparable to Amazon S-1 (0.1742)
   - Defense contractor language NOT as compressed as expected
   - Significantly higher than Blackstone "born caged" (0.1412)

2. SHANNON ENTROPY: LOWER than tech companies (9.2 vs 11-12)
   - Text is MORE PREDICTABLE despite decent vocabulary breadth
   - Suggests formulaic structure even with varied words
   - Pattern: Same ideas, different words vs. tech's different ideas, varied words

3. HEDGING TRAJECTORY: +48% increase (12.0 → 17.8/1k words)
   - Consistent year-over-year hedging escalation
   - Indicative of increasing legal/regulatory caution
   - 2015-2017: +24% jump (largest single increase)
   - This is the PREDICTIVE signal - hedging as leading indicator

4. 2019 ANOMALY: "Strategic Pivot" Effect
   - LD expansion (+14.6%) driven by Orbital ATK acquisition (June 2018)
   - 52 mentions of "orbital atk", 39 "acquisition", 38 "merger"
   - Matches Netflix DVD→streaming pivot pattern
   - Demonstrates: Major strategic change temporarily breaks compression

5. NOMINALIZATION: Stable at 65-70/1k words
   - Already at "bureaucratic baseline" - defense contractors born caged
   - No significant movement - structural language already formalized

HYPOTHESIS IMPLICATIONS
-----------------------
Q: Does the Variance Compression Thesis extend to defense contractors?

FINDING: Partially. Different pattern than tech companies.
- LD compression: MINIMAL (defense already "born caged" on vocabulary)
- SE compression: ALREADY LOW (formulaic from baseline)
- Hedging: SIGNIFICANT INCREASE (48%) - main variance signal
- The "cage" manifests differently:
  * Tech: Vocabulary compresses post-IPO
  * Defense: Vocabulary stable, but hedging/caution escalates
  
The government contracting environment pre-installs bureaucratic vocabulary,
but the fiduciary pressure still manifests through increasing epistemic hedging.
The HEDGING trajectory (+48%) is the actionable predictive signal for defense.

Q: Predictive or Symptomatic?

PRELIMINARY: Hedging appears LEADING.
- Hedging density increased steadily BEFORE any identified operational issues
- The 24% hedging jump in 2015-2017 preceded the strategic necessity of
  the Orbital ATK acquisition (diversification into solid rocket motors)
- Requires correlation with operational outcomes (cost overruns, schedule slips)
  to confirm predictive power
